Terrain Studio brush derivatives

These GLBs are modified from the source models: selected individual variants, grounded pivots, smaller embedded PBR textures with original alpha maps, and simplified distance levels. Pine needle geometry is replaced with the source twig photograph on cutout cards sampled across its original canopy. These changes are by the Terrain Studio asset builder, not the original authors. The unmodified archives remain in assets/cc0-foliage. Source URLs, author credits, CC0 license and modification notes are also embedded in each GLB.
